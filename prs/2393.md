# #2393 - trace-call matcher for reverted and internal-call triggers

- PR: https://github.com/KeeperHub/keeperhub/pull/2393
- Issue: #2241
- State: **merged**
- Area: event tracker

## What it does

The foundation of the trace-trigger line of work: a matcher that classifies reverted and
internal calls so workflows can trigger on them, not just on top-level logs. #2469 wires
this matcher into the event tracker.

## Proof through KeeperHub

- Transaction (Sepolia, status 0x1): https://sepolia.etherscan.io/tx/0x6bcf58d1758b34a098a1a1dfc2f84a818f7cc8c7b788d6d1e0b77b57f9d642a9
- Behaviour: the trace-matcher unit suite passes. See [../VERIFY.md](../VERIFY.md).

## Why it matters

Reverted and internal calls are invisible to a plain log filter. This matcher is what lets
KeeperHub fire a workflow on them, which #2469 then exposes as a Trace trigger.
